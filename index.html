About

I work on Qur'anic hermeneutics and vernacular tafsīr, with a focus on the Arabic-Malayalam scholarly tradition of the Mappila Muslims of Kerala and the Indian Ocean networks that carried it.

I am a Visiting Researcher at the Center for Islamic Theology, University of Tübingen, hosted by Prof. Ruggero Vimercati Sanseverino. I received my PhD in Theology and Qur'anic Studies from Necmettin Erbakan University in 2026, supervised by Prof. Dr. Ali Akpınar.

Research interests

  • Qur'anic hermeneutics and the history of tafsīr
  • Vernacular Qur'an translation and commentary
  • Arabic-Malayalam manuscripts and Mappila intellectual history
  • Indian Ocean Islamic scholarly networks
